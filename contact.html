Quick answers

Contact & Free Trial FAQs

How can I contact Online Quran ilm Academy?
You can message us on WhatsApp at +92 319 8427973, email info@onlinequranilmacademy.com, call the same number, or send the contact form on this page. Support is available 24/7.

How do I book a free trial Quran class?
Send us a message with the student's age, level and your country, or use the Book Free Trial button. We will arrange a live 1-on-1 trial class at a convenient time. No credit card is required.

Do you offer online Quran classes in the UK, USA, Canada and Australia?
Yes. Classes are live 1-on-1 video lessons, so families in the UK, USA, Canada, Australia and the rest of the world can join. Timings are arranged around your time zone.

Can I choose a male or female Quran teacher?
Yes. Both male and female certified tutors are available. Tell us your preference in your message and we will match you with a suitable teacher.

What do I need for an online Quran class?
A phone, tablet or computer with a camera and microphone, a stable internet connection and a quiet place to learn. We guide you through the setup before the first class.

Where can I see your fees?
Our monthly fees are on the Fee / Hadya page in USD, GBP, EUR, CAD and AUD, including the family discount.
