🗺️ Tarbiyah & Learning Roadmap

Milestone-based progression for steady spiritual growth

  • Level 1 (Foundation): Arabic Alphabets, Makharij points, and letter joining (Noorani Qaida).
  • Level 2 (Fluent Nazra): Direct Quranic reading with Tajweed (Noon/Meem Sakinah, Ghunnah, Qalqalah).
  • Level 3 (Tarteel & Breath Management): Melodious pace, sentence pauses (Waqf), and voice confidence.
  • Level 4 (Hifz & Revision): Systematic memorization of short Surahs or full Quran with tracking.
  • Level 5 (Tafseer & Duas): Word-by-word meaning, daily Sunnah supplications, and Salah training.

30 Juz Structured Progression Pipeline
