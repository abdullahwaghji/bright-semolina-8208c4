Importance & Audience

Who is this course designed for?

Learning Quran recitation with proper Tajweed is obligatory for every Muslim to perform daily prayers correctly. This program is specially curated for beginners, children starting their Quranic journey, and adults looking to fix pronunciation flaws.

Target Audience:

Kids ages 4+ and adults of all proficiency levels.

Prerequisites:

No prior Arabic knowledge required for beginners.

Structured Course Modules

Step-by-step learning milestones

  • Module 1: Arabic Alphabets pronunciation & Makharij.
  • Module 2: Harakaat, Tanween, and Letter Connections.
  • Module 3: Rules of Noon Sakinah, Meem Sakinah & Madd.
  • Module 4: Fluent Recitation of selected Surahs & Juz Amma.
