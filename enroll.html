Enrolment & Free Trial FAQs

How do I enroll in online Quran classes?

Fill out the short form on this page. Our coordinator will contact you by email, WhatsApp or phone to arrange a free 1-on-1 trial session. If you are happy with it, you are registered as a regular student.

Is the trial class really free?

Yes. New students get 3 free trial classes. Book one to meet a tutor, check the audio and video, and see how your child feels about learning. No card details are needed.

What age can my child start learning the Quran online?

Most children start between ages 4 and 6 with Noorani Qaida. Older children and adults are welcome too, and your tutor will begin at the level that suits the student.

What do we need to join online classes?

A computer, tablet or phone with a camera and microphone, a stable internet connection and a quiet corner. Headphones help children focus. We will guide you through the setup before the first class.

Can my daughter learn with a female tutor?

Yes. Female tutors are available for girls and for women. Select your preference in the form or mention it when we contact you.

Do you teach students in the UK, USA, Canada and Australia?

Yes. Classes are live online, so students in the UK, USA, Canada, Australia and worldwide can join. Timings are arranged around your time zone.

How are class timings arranged?

You choose the days and time slots that fit school, work and your time zone. If something changes, you can ask to reschedule with advance notice.

How much do the classes cost?

Plans start from $40 per month and are available in USD, GBP, EUR, CAD and AUD, with a family discount for siblings. See the Fee / Hadya page for every plan.

Which payment methods do you accept?

We accept Visa, Mastercard, PayPal and JazzCash.

How will I know my child is making progress?

Parents receive a progress report every month covering what was learned, strengths and areas to practise. Tutors also share short feedback after lessons.
