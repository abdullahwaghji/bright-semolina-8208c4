Fees explained

Online Quran Classes Fees — FAQs

Is there a free trial?
Yes. Every plan starts with 3 free trial classes so you can meet a tutor and see how your child learns. No credit card is required.

When do I need to pay for my classes?
We process monthly fees in advance during the first week of every month. This simple routine secures your spot in class and ensures our schedule runs smoothly for everyone.

Which currency and payment methods can I use?
Fees are available in USD, GBP, EUR, CAD and AUD, so you can choose the currency for your region. Our team confirms the available payment methods when you enroll.

Is there a family or sibling discount?
Yes. The Starter plan has a 5% family discount and the Standard, Regular and Fast-track plans have a 10% family discount for additional siblings. Use the fee calculator on this page for an estimate.

How much do online Quran classes cost?
At Online Quran ilm Academy, live 1-on-1 online Quran classes start at $40 per month (Starter, 2 classes a week). Standard is $55, Regular is $65 and Fast-track is $80 per month. Prices are also listed in GBP, EUR, CAD and AUD, and include all learning materials.

What are the Quran class fees in the UK?
For families in the UK, fees are charged in British Pounds: £35 (Starter), £40 (Standard), £50 (Regular) and £55 (Fast-track) per month.

What are the online Quran class fees in the USA, Canada and Australia?
USA and worldwide: $40, $55, $65 and $80 per month. Canada: C$60, C$70, C$80 and C$100. Australia: A$60, A$70, A$80 and A$100. Plans are Starter, Standard, Regular and Fast-track respectively.

What does the monthly fee (hadya) include?
Live 1-on-1 classes with a certified Quran tutor, 30-minute lessons, a choice of preferred tutors, a monthly progress report, an e-certificate and all learning materials.

How long is each class and how many classes do I get?
Each lesson is 30 minutes of live 1-on-1 teaching. Starter gives 2 classes a week (8 lessons a month), Standard 3 (12), Regular 4 (16) and Fast-track 5 (20).

Which plan should I choose for my child?
Most children do well on the Standard plan (3 classes a week). Choose Starter for a lighter schedule, or Regular and Fast-track for faster progress, such as Hifz or Tajweed revision. The tutor can advise you after the free trial.

Can I get a custom plan?
Yes. If your schedule or goals need something different, message us on WhatsApp and we will suggest a custom plan.
