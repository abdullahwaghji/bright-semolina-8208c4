Our Story

Traditional Quranic Learning, Delivered Through Modern Online Education

What is Online Quran ilm Academy? It is an online Quran school where students learn to read, recite and understand the Holy Quran in live one-to-one video classes with certified tutors, from home, at times that suit their family.

We serve Muslim families in the UK, USA, Canada, Australia, New Zealand, Ireland and across the world. Whether you want online Quran classes for kids, a certified female Quran teacher for your daughter or yourself, Noorani Qaida for a beginner, a Hifz programme, or Arabic grammar to understand the Quran, each lesson is planned around the student’s level, age and pace.

Busy schedules and long commutes should not stand between a child and the Quran. That is why classes are live, private and flexible, so you can learn Quran with Tajweed from the comfort of home.

Good to know

About Our Online Quran Academy FAQs

What is the best online Quran academy for kids in the UK, USA and Canada?
Online Quran ilm Academy offers live 1-on-1 Quran classes with certified male and female tutors for children in the UK, USA, Canada, Australia and worldwide. Every family can book free trial classes first to judge the teaching for themselves.

Who teaches at Online Quran ilm Academy?
Certified Quran tutors, both male and female. Tutors are checked and tested for Quranic proficiency before they teach students, and you can choose a preferred tutor.

Do you offer female Quran teachers online?
Yes. Certified female Quran teachers are available for sisters, girls and young children. Mention your preference when you enroll.

Can adults and new Muslims (reverts) learn the Quran with you?
Yes. Adults and reverts can start from the Arabic letters with Noorani Qaida, learn at their own pace in private classes, then continue to Tajweed, translation or Arabic.

What courses do you offer?
Noorani Qaida, Quran Reading with Tajweed, Quran Memorization (Hifz), Quran Translation & Tafseer, Islamic Studies & Daily Duas, and Arabic Language.

How do the online classes work?
Classes are live one-to-one video lessons in a secure virtual classroom with screen sharing and live audio feedback. You only need a device with a camera and microphone and a stable internet connection.

Do you have a free trial?
Yes. New students get 3 free trial classes with no credit card required, so you can meet a tutor before choosing a plan.

How much do the classes cost?
Plans start from $40 per month and are available in USD, GBP, EUR, CAD and AUD, with a family discount for siblings. Full details are on our Fee / Hadya page.

Which time zones do you support?
All of them. Class times are arranged around your time zone, school and work, and support is available 24/7.
