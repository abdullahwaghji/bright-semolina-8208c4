A Female Quran Tutor Who Fits Your Family

Quick answer

Online Quran ilm Academy offers private live Quran classes with certified female teachers for girls, sisters, mothers and new Muslim women. Choose Noorani Qaida, Tajweed, Hifz, Tafseer or Arabic, learn at times that suit your time zone, and start with 3 free trial classes.

Many Muslim families prefer a female teacher for daughters, and many women feel more at ease learning with another woman. Whether you are returning to the Quran after years, starting from the Arabic letters, or looking for a patient first teacher for your child, a female tutor can make learning calmer and more consistent.

Every lesson is one-to-one, so your teacher listens closely, corrects gently in the moment, and adapts the pace to you. Parents are welcome to sit in on a child’s lesson.

Benefits of a female teacher

  • A comfortable, modest learning environment at home
  • Peace of mind for parents of girls
  • Easier to ask questions on personal topics like women’s fiqh basics
  • No pressure from a group, at your own pace

Good to know

Female Quran Teacher FAQs

Do you offer female Quran teachers online?

Yes. Certified female Quran teachers are available for girls, sisters, mothers and young children. Mention your preference when you book your free trial.

Will my classes always be with a female teacher?

Yes, if you request a female teacher we assign one for your regular lessons, subject to availability for your course and time zone.

Can my daughter learn Quran with a female teacher?

Yes. Many parents choose a female teacher for their daughters. Lessons are one-to-one and a parent is welcome to sit in, especially for younger children.

Are the classes private?

Yes. Every class is a live one-to-one session in a secure virtual classroom, so there is no group to keep up with.

Can adult women and new Muslims join?

Yes. Adult women and new Muslims can start from the Arabic letters with Noorani Qaida and learn at their own pace, then continue to Tajweed, Hifz, Tafseer or Arabic.

What can I study with a female teacher?

Noorani Qaida, Quran reading with Tajweed, Quran memorization (Hifz), Quran translation and Tafseer, Islamic studies and daily duas, and Arabic language, subject to teacher availability for each course.

Can I change my teacher if it is not a good fit?

Yes. If the first match does not suit you or your child, tell us and we will arrange another available teacher.

Is there a free trial with a female teacher?

Yes. New students get 3 free trial classes with no credit card required, and you can ask for a female teacher from the first class.
