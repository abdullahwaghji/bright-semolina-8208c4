Course Overview

Connecting Western Muslim Families with Arabic

For Muslim children and adults growing up in Western societies, learning Arabic is the key to directly connecting with Islamic heritage, understanding daily Salah, and comprehending the Quran without relying solely on translations.

At Online Quran ilm Academy, our Modern Standard Arabic (Fusha) and Quranic Arabic course is structured specifically for non-native speakers. Our native Arabic scholars use immersive speech practice, visual tools, and gentle step-by-step guidance to build complete confidence in reading, writing, and speaking Arabic.

Key Highlights of Our Arabic Program

Tailored to help beginner and intermediate students master Arabic quickly and naturally:

Conversational Fluency: Speak daily Arabic expressions confidently with native speakers.
Quranic Vocabulary Mastery: Understand the most common words and root structures of the Mushaf.
Simplified Grammar (Nahw & Sarf): Learn sentence formation without tedious memorization.
Revert-Friendly Atmosphere: Patient, supportive environment for new Muslims starting from zero.

Step-by-Step Arabic Modules

From alphabet recognition to fluent conversational sentences

  • Module 1: Arabic alphabet phonetics, correct articulation points (Makharij), and writing rules.
  • Module 2: High-frequency everyday vocabulary, family terms, numbers, and Quranic root words.
  • Module 3: Basic sentence construction, pronouns, adjectives, and verb conjugations.
  • Module 4: Interactive dialogue practice, listening comprehension, and reading short passages.
